✏️ Grammar Rules
قواعد اللغة الإنجليزية
1. Is there a ...? / Are there any ...?
هل يوجد ...؟ / هل يوجد أي ...؟

Singular (مفرد):

❓ Is there a park in your street? → Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.
❓ Is there a supermarket near here? → Yes, there is!

Plural (جمع):

❓ Are there any bus stops? → Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.

نستخدم Is there a مع الأسماء المفردة (واحد فقط)

نستخدم Are there any مع الأسماء الجمع (أكثر من واحد)

الإجابة بالإيجاب: Yes, there is / Yes, there are

الإجابة بالنفي: No, there isn't / No, there aren't

2. Where's the ...? — Prepositions
أين ...؟ — حروف الجر
❓ Where's the car? → It's next to the lorry.
❓ Where's the motorbike? → It's between the car and the van.
❓ Where's the van? → It's in front of the car.
❓ Where's the bike? → It's behind the bus.

next to = بجانب

between = بين (شيئين)

in front of = أمام

behind = خلف

3. What's he/she doing? — Present Continuous
ماذا يفعل هو/هي؟ — المضارع المستمر
❓ What's he doing? → He's playing chess.
❓ What's she doing? → She's playing badminton.
❓ What are they doing? → They're playing tag.
❓ Is he reading comics? → Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.

نستخدم المضارع المستمر للتعبير عن أفعال تحدث الآن

am/is/are + verb + ing

He's = He is | She's = She is | They're = They are

مثال: He is playing → يلعب الآن

4. I like / I don't like + verb-ing
أنا أحب / لا أحب + فعل + ing
✅ I like skipping.
❌ I don't like skateboarding.
❓ Do you like hopping? → Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
✅ She likes bouncing. He likes bouncing, too!
❓ Does he like skipping? → Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.

بعد like/likes نضع الفعل مضافاً إليه ing

للجمع ولـ I: like / don't like

للمفرد (he/she): likes / doesn't like

السؤال مع I/you/we/they: Do you like ...?

السؤال مع he/she: Does he/she like ...?

5. I'm good at + verb-ing
أنا جيد في + فعل + ing
✅ I'm good at painting pictures.
❓ Are you good at juggling? → Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
✅ He's good at rollerblading.
✅ She's good at acting.

بعد good at نضع الفعل مضافاً إليه ing

I'm good at = أنا جيد في

He/She is good at = هو/هي جيد/جيدة في

الإجابة بالإيجاب: Yes, I am / Yes, he does

الإجابة بالنفي: No, I'm not / No, he doesn't

🔤 Prefixes & Suffixes
السوابق واللواحق
English

Prefixes are added at the beginning of a word. Suffixes are added at the end.

العربية

السوابق تُضاف في بداية الكلمة. اللواحق تُضاف في نهاية الكلمة.

🔡 Verb Forms
أشكال الفعل
  • play → playing → plays
  • ride → riding (drop the e!)
  • hop → hopping (double consonant!)
  • skip → skipping (double consonant!)
  • do → doing
  • watch → watching
  • listen → listening
⚠️ Short verb + vowel + consonant → double last letter + ing
فعل قصير + مقطع صائت + حرف صامت = ضاعف آخر حرف + ing
📝 Sentence Building
بناء الجمل
  • Subject + is/are + verb-ing
  • There + is/are + a/any + noun
  • Subject + like/likes + verb-ing
  • Subject + is/am/are + good at + verb-ing
  • Where + is/are + the + noun?
  • It's + preposition + the + noun
✅ Punctuation Tips
قواعد علامات الترقيم
  • Start sentences with capital letter
  • End questions with ? mark
  • End statements with . period
  • Use ' in contractions (isn't, I'm)
  • Names always start with capitals
  • Days/months start with capitals
